Explore UNITY SPACE

Why we exist, and where we are heading.

Our mission

To make powered landings routine. We give every engineering team a reusable, modular VTVL test platform, so drones and rocket stages can be flown, landed and flown again without building a new test stand each time.

Our vision

A future where reusable flight is the default. Any team, from a university lab to a launch startup, takes a vehicle from its first hop to a precise landing on hardware that is built to be reused.

UNITY SPACE · Projects

Our Projects

From experimental rockets to reusable vertical flight platforms, our projects are built around practical aerospace engineering and flight testing.

CHETAK

Experimental Rocket Development

Our vision

Building Through Experimentation

To develop practical rocketry capabilities through experimentation, engineering, testing and continuous learning.

Our mission

Engineering Beyond Theory

To design, develop and test experimental rocket systems while building hands-on aerospace engineering experience.

Development Series · 01

CHETAK 1

The first vehicle in the CHETAK series, built to establish the basic rocket architecture, airframe and flight hardware.

Development Series · 02

CHETAK 2

Builds on CHETAK 1 with improved structure, electronics and recovery, and includes the MK-I and MK-II flight demonstrations.

Development Series · 03

CHETAK 3

The latest development stage with parachute ejection, bringing together the lessons from earlier flights.

DELTA

Thrust Vector Control Development

Our vision

Advancing Flight Control

To explore advanced rocket control technologies and improve the understanding of flight stability and directional control.

Our mission

Developing Precision Systems

To design and experiment with a servo-actuated thrust vector control mechanism for experimental rocket systems.

HELIX

Flight Computer and Telemetry Systems

Our vision

Intelligent Aerospace Systems

To build reliable electronic and computational systems that support experimental rocket development and flight testing.

Our mission

Connecting Data With Flight

To develop flight computer, telemetry and data acquisition systems for monitoring and understanding rocket flight behaviour.

HELIX · FLIGHT TESTING

Flight Demonstration

HELIX develops flight-computer and telemetry systems to monitor experimental rocket flights and support post-test data review.

HELIX · DESIGN

CAD Render

The CAD layout reviews packaging and interfaces for flight-computer, sensor and telemetry hardware used in rocket testing.

Team · About

About Unity Space

Our story

Turning curiosity into aerospace.

Unity Space is an aerospace innovation initiative developing reusable, modular VTVL testing platforms for drones and rockets.

We aim to make aerospace testing more affordable, accessible and efficient by enabling teams to test and validate individual technologies without building an entirely new vehicle for every experiment.

Our vision is to accelerate India's aerospace innovation through indigenous engineering, reusable technology and data-driven testing.
